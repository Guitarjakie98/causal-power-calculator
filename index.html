What this calculator assumes

Independent units, a binary outcome, two randomized groups, a two-sided test, and a normal approximation for the difference in proportions. Clustered, repeated-measures, geo, and switchback designs need different variance assumptions.

Built for experiment planning. Power calculations depend on assumptions; document them before looking at results.
